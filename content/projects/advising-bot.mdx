## The problem

Degree requirements, prerequisites, and policy exceptions are spread across documents that
students and advisors both have to read carefully. The answers exist — they are just expensive
to look up, and getting one wrong costs a semester.

## What I built

- **Frontend.** A Next.js and Tailwind interface for asking degree planning questions.
- **Backend.** A Python Flask service that ingests degree requirements and policies to
  streamline advising workflows.
- **Retrieval.** A semantic vector store built on Pinecone and Supabase, indexing scraped
  institutional data so answers resolve against real source material.
- **Grounding.** LLM summarization under strict context grounding, so recommendations stay
  concise and tied to what was actually retrieved.
- **Ingestion.** Automated scraping and ingestion, cutting the manual lookup time advisors
  would otherwise spend.

## Why it matters

This is the clearest expression of what I want to do: take an unstructured, high-stakes domain
and turn it into a system a real person would trust with a real decision.
